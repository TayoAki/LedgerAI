# Ledger

Ledger is software that bookkeeping Firms subscribe to so that AI agents do the routine work of keeping their Clients' books, while the Firm's own accountant signs off.

## Language

**Firm**:
A bookkeeping or accounting practice that subscribes to Ledger and keeps books for its own Clients.
_Avoid_: Customer, tenant, account, owner

**Client**:
A business whose books a Firm keeps.
_Avoid_: Customer, user, company
